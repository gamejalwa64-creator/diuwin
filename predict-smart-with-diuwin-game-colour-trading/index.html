The Colour Options on the DiuWin app

Genuine tips for first-time players on DiuWin games

These are some clever pointers to colour forecast in the Diuwin app, so to minimize loss, follow these tips:

1. Notice the Pattern, Do not Chase It

A lot of players fall into the wrong belief of betting on the last result- e.g., green came three times, so now red will come. Rather, sit and calmly decide your next move.

2. The first time, just stick to Green or Red

Violet is rare, so do not bet so much on violet early. When you are not yet conversant with the rhythm of the platform, stick to green and red.

3. Apply 3x Strategy

It is a very well-known technique that can be expressed as the following rule: when you receive a loss, you increase your bet slightly and do the opposite, i.e., go back to the initial amount when you win. It is risky and has the potential to make up for past losses. Consume with caution and put a limitation on how much to play each day.

4. Decide on a Budget and Exit point

Pre-determine how much you will play in a day and decide a figure that you will quit playing even when you are playing on a winning streak. It is also a game of numbers and even a game of the mind.
