Common problems & quick fixes

  • Withdrawal pending for a long time: Confirm KYC is approved and that you used the exact UPI ID or bank details. If details were wrong, contact support immediately.
  • Transaction shows "successful" but money has not been received: Wait one banking cycle (a few hours to 24 hours). If still missing, share the transaction ID with Diuwin support and your bank.
  • Withdrawal rejected due to bonuses or wagering rules: Some bonus balances are non-withdrawable until wagering criteria are met. Read the bonus T&C in the app.

How to contact support

Use the in-app support/chat first; it is the fastest way to send transaction IDs and screenshots. If in-app support is slow, check the platform's official social handles or support pages for alternate contact methods. Keep screenshots and timestamps handy when you reach out.

Smart tips to avoid delays
